Zentro Labs Employee Handbook

Leave Policy
Every employee gets 24 paid leave days per year. Unused leave can be carried forward, but only up to 10 days. Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the Zentro portal.

Work From Home
Employees can work from home up to 3 days per week. Fridays are mandatory office days for all teams. Exceptions need written approval from the team manager.

Laptop and Equipment
Every new joiner receives a laptop within 2 days of joining. If a laptop is lost, the employee must report it to the IT desk within 24 hours. Replacement takes up to 7 working days.

Reimbursements
Internet bill reimbursement is capped at 1000 rupees per month. Claims must be filed before the 5th of the following month with a valid bill attached.

Probation
The probation period is 3 months. Performance is reviewed at the end of month 2, and confirmation letters are sent within a week of the final review.
